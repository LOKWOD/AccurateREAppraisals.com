Frequently asked questions

What information do you need to quote an appraisal?

Usually the property address, the reason the appraisal is needed, the client or intended user, and any effective date or deadline that matters. Complex properties may require a little more information before scope and fee can be confirmed.

How quickly can an appraisal be completed?

Timing depends on the property, assignment type, access, effective date and current scheduling. We confirm expected timing with the scope and fee rather than publishing a one-size-fits-all turnaround promise.

Do you handle retrospective or date-of-death appraisals?

Yes. Estate and other legal or financial matters may require a value opinion as of a prior effective date. The required date should be identified by the attorney, accountant, executor or other professional directing the assignment.

Can a homeowner order an appraisal for a mortgage or refinance?

For lending use, the lender or authorized lending client generally controls the appraisal ordering process. Homeowners can contact us with questions, but an independently ordered report may not satisfy a lender's requirements.

Do you appraise waterfront, acreage and unusual homes?

Yes. Complex residential assignments can include waterfront property, rural acreage, custom construction, accessory buildings and other properties where the competitive market may extend beyond the immediate neighborhood.
