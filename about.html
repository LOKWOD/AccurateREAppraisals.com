Valuation, not a sales pitch.

An appraisal is an independent opinion of value developed for an identified client, intended use, effective date and scope of work. Our role is to analyze the property and the market evidence relevant to that assignment.

Local context matters.

Central New York ranges from city neighborhoods and subdivisions to villages, rural acreage, waterfront property and custom homes. Market boundaries are not always the same as ZIP codes, postal names or municipal lines.

Residential property types

  • single-family residences
  • small residential income properties where appropriate
  • vacant residential land
  • waterfront and water-influenced property
  • rural acreage and custom homes
