Appraisal Services

Independent residential valuation for legal, financial, lending and private decisions across Central New York.

Different decisions require different appraisal assignments. Start with why the value is needed; the intended use, effective date and property type determine the appropriate scope.

How We Scope Work

Property + purpose + effective date.

1

Property

Address, property type and anything unusually complex about the site or improvements.

2

Purpose

The decision the appraisal needs to support and the identified client or intended users.

3

Date

Current effective date or a specific historical date when the assignment is retrospective.

Not Sure Which Service?
