APPRAISAL GUIDE

Appraisal vs. CMA

An appraisal is an independent valuation assignment; a CMA is generally a brokerage tool used for listing or purchase strategy. Both may use comparable sales, but they serve different roles.

When the distinction matters

Estate, divorce, tax, private transfer and other decisions can require an independent valuation rather than a marketing recommendation.

Start with the valuation question

The property, intended use, intended users and effective date determine the appropriate scope of work. If you are unsure what type of appraisal you need, send the address and explain the decision the value needs to support.
