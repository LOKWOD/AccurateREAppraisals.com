APPRAISAL GUIDE

How to Prepare for a Residential Appraisal

The best preparation is straightforward access and accurate property information—not staging the house like a listing photo shoot.

What helps the appraiser

Provide access to the property and major improvements, and identify significant updates, unusual site features, acreage, waterfront rights or accessory buildings.

Start with the valuation question

The property, intended use, intended users and effective date determine the appropriate scope of work. If you are unsure what type of appraisal you need, send the address and explain the decision the value needs to support.

Need an independent value opinion?

We will review the assignment before confirming scope, fee and scheduling.
