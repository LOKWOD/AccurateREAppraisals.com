APPRAISAL GUIDE

Understanding the Effective Date

Every appraisal has an effective date. A retrospective assignment asks what the property was worth on a prior date, so the relevant market evidence and property condition must be considered as of that time.

Historical assignments

Estate, litigation and tax-related matters may require historical valuation. The client or professional adviser should identify the required effective date.

Start with the valuation question

The property, intended use, intended users and effective date determine the appropriate scope of work. If you are unsure what type of appraisal you need, send the address and explain the decision the value needs to support.

We will review the assignment before confirming scope, fee and scheduling.
