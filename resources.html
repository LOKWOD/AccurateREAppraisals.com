Property Research

Official property, assessment, tax, flood-map and appraisal resources.

These external resources are useful starting points for Central New York property research. County and agency websites control their own data and procedures.

Use county property records to confirm parcel and assessment information, state resources for tax and licensing questions, and FEMA for official flood-map research. External links open in a new tab.
